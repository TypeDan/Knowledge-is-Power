# Question-bank notes

All questions and distractors were written for this mod. The bank covers science, geography, history, literature, arts, sport, mathematics, food, computing, games, language and Christmas. It uses factual questions rather than copying a commercial quiz bank or reproducing song lyrics. Regional Christmas customs are identified where relevant. The standard adult skeleton, sea-level boiling point, traditional recipes and Gregorian calendar are qualified explicitly.

Specialist facts checked against primary or authoritative sources:

- The eight-reindeer poem: [A Visit from St. Nicholas, Poetry Foundation](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/43171/a-visit-from-st-nicholas).
- Christmas market location: [Nuremberg Christkindlesmarkt](https://www.christkindlesmarkt.de/en/).
- Italian festive figure: [Italian national tourism board: Befana traditions](https://www.italia.it/en/italy/things-to-do/epiphany-in-italy-befana-meaning-and-traditions).
- Linguine meaning: [Barilla's product description](https://www.barilla.com/en-us/products/pasta/classic-blue-box/linguine).

The game uses an offline, synchronized shuffled deck. It cannot guarantee that different matches never share any questions: each match draws afresh from the same large bank. It guarantees no repeat within a match until all enabled questions have been used. Christmas-only mode has its own 104-question pool; general-only mode has 300.
