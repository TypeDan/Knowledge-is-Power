# Knowledge is Power — 2026.11.23.3

Each team now has one shared pass for the entire match. The chosen answerer can press Pass (1) before the 20-second deadline to skip that question with no reward or penalty. Passes never refill. Teammates see when the pass is used, and answerer changes and saved-state restoration do not restore it.

Wrong answers now split one team penalty equal to one-third of the prize. In Age I, each member of a two-player team pays 50 of each resource; each member of a four-player team pays 25. Both the prize and penalty use the answering player's age when the round opens. Charges are shown in advance and fixed for that round. The unaffordable-bill penalty remains 10% of the player's living villagers, rounded up.

AI-only teams get the same single pass and use it when a wrong answer would risk villagers. They do not inspect their hidden correct/incorrect result to make that decision. Answered AI questions retain their 50% success chance.

The three-minute question interval, teammate suggestions, question bank, rewards and project GUID are unchanged. A Simplified Chinese multiplier label now uses the normal Chinese wording for three times, resolving a store moderation rejection.

Published in the [AoE IV catalogue as listing 594036](https://www.ageofempires.com/mods/details/594036/). AoE created a new listing; the superseded 594034 listing has been removed. Existing players should subscribe to the new listing. The store download matches this release archive byte-for-byte. See VALIDATION.md for automated and native test results and their limits.
